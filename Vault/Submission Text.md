**Title:** Mission Crew: Ice Return

**One-line pitch:** A four-player co-op game where a crew builds a spacecraft, flies it to the Moon, lands near the South Pole, drills for ice, protects the samples and brings them home. Every player has a different job and different information, so nobody can finish the mission alone.

**Challenge:** [exact 2026 challenge title]

**What it is:** Six stages, each with four roles: (1) Design & Assembly in a 3D vertical assembly bay (choose launcher, build lander and ascent stack, live mass/power/thrust/fuel/budget checks), (2) Launch & Transit with a 3D chase view of the ship and timed course corrections, (3) Orbit & Landing (site ranking, hazard marking, descent), (4) Surface exploration (rover, drill, shared battery power), (5) Sample preservation (label, seal, cool, secure), (6) Return to Earth and a debrief screen with per-player accuracy.

**Why it matters / who benefits:** it teaches the real order and trade-offs of a lunar sample-return mission and the communication discipline of mission control. Intended for students, outreach and team-training.

**Science and data (real, simplified for play):** NASA 3D Resources models (Space Shuttle boosters, Saturn V parts, Mission Control, Stardust capsule), LRO/LOLA lunar topography, Blue Marble and Black Marble Earth imagery, Perseverance rover model, Apollo Lunar Module, public launcher payload figures. Game numbers are simplified and flagged as such in the repo.

**Technology:** Unreal Engine 5.8, C++, LAN listen-server multiplayer for 4 players, data-driven parts and presets.
